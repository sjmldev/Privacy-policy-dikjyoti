Welcome to Dikjyoti Online Test ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and providing a secure environment for students and teachers. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Android mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly necessary to provide educational test delivery, evaluation, and account administration:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you register as a student or educator, we collect your name, email address, phone number, role (Student or Teacher), and selected batch.
  • Academic & Performance Data: Examination submissions, answers, scores, timestamps, test completion duration, and leaderboard points.
  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Device model, Android OS version, application version, crash logs, and performance metrics collected to maintain app reliability.
  • Authentication Credentials: Securely handled password hashes and user authentication tokens via Firebase Authentication.
Strict Privacy Guarantee: We do not track or store your precise GPS location, contacts, or financial details. Coarse region data is used solely for regional language and service routing.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for legitimate educational purposes, including:

  • Enabling students to take online exams, review instant answer explanations, and view score analytics.
  • Enabling teachers to publish questions, administer mock tests, and review student performance reports.
  • Maintaining fair leaderboards, streaks, and achievement badges.
  • Sending critical test schedule notifications and announcements via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Preventing abuse, exam malpractice, and unauthorized account access.

3. Advertising & Monetization (Ads)

In-App Advertisements Notice:

This application displays advertisements (such as Banner Ads and optional Rewarded Video Ads) to support free educational test series for students.

We use certified third-party ad networks, including Google AdMob. These ad partners may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID) to serve ads in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

Users can reset their Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android Device Settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers to deliver reliable services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): For secure user sign-in and encrypted cloud database storage.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): For delivering timely test alerts and announcements.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements within the app.
  • Google Play Services: For core application runtime and update management.

Please review Google's Privacy & Terms for details on how Google processes data.

5. Device Permissions Required

The app requests the following minimal Android permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To connect to test servers, sync results, and load exam questions.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To deliver exam reminders, test publication alerts, and announcement notifications.
  • VIBRATE: For tactile haptic feedback during quiz submissions and button presses.

6. Data Security

We implement robust administrative and technical security measures. All communications between the app and cloud servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). User passwords and sensitive authentication tokens are handled securely and never stored in plain text.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies, you have complete control over your data:

  • You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated exam records, scores, and personal data at any time.
  • To request account or data deletion, simply send an email to shajamalali675@gmail.com or contact us at shajamal.live with your registered email address.
  • Your account and data will be permanently wiped from our active databases within 7 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are designed for educational test preparation. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 without verified parental or educational guardian consent. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information without consent, we take immediate steps to delete such data.
